# Pronunciation content review for IPA365 Flashcards

Updated **31 October 2026** against **v1.1.0-preview.2**. Each entry below identifies the exact symbol and full label, quotes the complete tip currently displayed by the app, and places its proposed change or example decision immediately beneath it. **No pronunciation corrections have been applied.**

There are **84 individual symbols** in this review: the union of 53 symbols with tip-review notes and 65 sounds with unresolved lexical examples. Each symbol appears once. Original A01–A24 references are retained so decisions can refer to those proposals.

The current tip and current displayed word example are separate fields. An original example candidate can still exist in the source data even when it is not shown in the preview; those candidates are explicitly labelled below. An issue with a stored candidate is not necessarily an error in the displayed tip. For combining marks, **◌** is a dotted-circle carrier used only to make the exact mark visible.

## How to review

Read the current quotation before accepting a proposal. Approve, amend or reject a change by symbol or its A-reference. For unresolved examples, specify a spelling/transliteration, broad transcription, meaning and language/variety. Production gestures and carrier demonstrations do not need invented lexical meanings.

The agreed lexical format is *spelling or transliteration* /broad transcription/, 'meaning' in a named language/variety. Phonetic realizations use [square brackets] in the tip. The proposed second step is to retain the production guidance and move embedded lexical comparisons into one fully formatted word example once its content is agreed.

**Proposed changes or review notes**

Do not give a replacement transcription until the variety is fixed. **Basis:** Swedish retroflexion commonly involves r plus a following coronal; this word needs checking. [Retroflex stop study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S009544700800003X).
- **A02:** Several cues imply that every retroflex uses the underside of a tightly curled tip. **Proposal:** “Raise and retract the tongue tip towards the postalveolar region. The degree of curling and the contact surface vary between languages.” Retain the manner-specific closure, nasal airflow, friction or lateral airflow instructions. **Basis:** Retroflex articulations vary; [research on Norwegian retroflex stops](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S009544700800003X).

**Example decision needed:** Replace the Swedish stor claim; choose a variety-specific retroflex example.
